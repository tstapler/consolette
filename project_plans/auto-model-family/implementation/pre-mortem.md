# Pre-mortem: auto-model-family

**Date**: 2026-09-12
**Premise**: The feature shipped, and one month later it has failed — either reverted to pins, distrusted, or causing spend/outages. This document lists the five most plausible ways that happened, and what to change now to prevent each.

## Failure Modes

| # | Failure (1 sentence) | First symptom (earliest observable) | Prevention (1 concrete change) | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Single-user volume (tens of reqs/day) makes error-rate ranking pure noise, so one stray 500 (1/8 = 12.5% vs 0/10) flips the pick and the family flaps between members on nothing. | Dashboard pick-change log shows A→B→A within a day on sample counts n<15 with margins inside noise. | Raise the minimum-sample threshold (N≥20, not 5) and require Wilson-interval non-overlap (or err delta >5pp at n<30) before stats override config order; below threshold serve config order flagged `cold`. (Epic 2 Story 2.2 + Epic 3 Story 3.3 constants.) | P1 |
| 2 | Winner-takes-all herding: 100% of family traffic hits the current pick, a free-tier model with the tightest rate limit, converting a latency edge into 429s, demotion, and oscillation between two exhausted members. | `fallback_to_default_total` spikes and both members sit in cooldown after the first multi-file-edit burst; `/metrics` shows 429s clustered on the pick. | Treat probe traffic as the only load-spreading mechanism is insufficient — add a per-member concurrency cap (default 2–4 in-flight) plus confirm the shared-upstream-429 test (Story 3.2 AC5) proves B stays eligible when A 429s, before enabling the family route. | P1 |
| 3 | Every OpenRouter `:free` rotation costs one user-visible failed request: the first request after a fresh delist hits the dead member, fails fast on `Validation(_, 404)` with no failover (accepted limitation, Story 3.2), and aborts the agent loop mid-task. | Claude Code/opencode surfaces a 404 model-not-found error to Tyler despite the family feature being on; denylist entry appears only after the failure. | Add a bounded try-next inside the family expansion for the `Validation(_, 404)` class only (attempt the next-ranked healthy member in the same request), or a lightweight background catalog/liveness refresh; document the residual risk if deferred. | P2 |
| 4 | Restart amnesia + hot-swap state confusion destroys trust: restarts silently reset rankings to config order while the dashboard implies a confident pick, and the 1h denylist/counters survive `POST /api/route` rollback into a membership where they no longer apply. | After a restart or rollback, the served model differs from the pre-restart pick with `window_age_s` near zero and no banner; or a denylisted member stays excluded after membership changed. | Dashboard must show `window_age_s` + per-member `n` next to every pick with an explicit low-confidence banner when under threshold, and scope/clear denylist entries whose `(upstream, model)` is no longer in the rebuilt `FamilyTable` on `post_route`. | P2 |
| 5 | Paid-alias leakage: a rotated ID without the `:free` suffix (or a stale vendored pricing snapshot) bills the free alias, violating the hard no-new-spend constraint silently. | `cost_metrics` shows non-zero spend attributed to `auto-coding` resolutions, or `paid resolutions` counter increments on the free alias. | Fail closed by default (unknown pricing + no `:free` suffix = reject in free families — already planned), PLUS add a runtime resolution-time re-check against the pricing table (not load-time only) and a `/metrics` paid-resolution alert banner on the free card. | P3 |
